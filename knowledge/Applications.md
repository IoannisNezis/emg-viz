# Applications of Surface EMG

## Clinical & Rehabilitation

### Biofeedback

Display real-time EMG amplitude to a patient so they can learn to activate (or relax) specific muscles. Used in:
- Pelvic floor rehabilitation
- Stroke recovery (relearning muscle activation)
- Chronic pain management (muscle relaxation training)
- Postural correction

### Gait Analysis

EMG of leg muscles during walking/running to identify:
- Abnormal activation timing
- Muscle compensation patterns
- Effects of orthotics or surgery
- Usually combined with motion capture and force plates

### Neuromuscular Assessment

- Measuring fatigue (median frequency shift during sustained contraction)
- Monitoring recovery after nerve injury
- Evaluating prosthetic socket fit

## Ergonomics & Occupational Health

- **Workstation assessment**: measuring muscle load during computer use, assembly work
- **Lifting biomechanics**: back muscle activation during manual handling
- **Fatigue monitoring**: tracking muscle fatigue over work shifts
- **Vehicle seating design**: muscle tension during driving

## Sports Science

- **Technique analysis**: which muscles activate, in what sequence, during athletic movements
- **Training load monitoring**: muscle activation intensity across training sessions
- **Injury prevention**: identifying muscle imbalances or abnormal firing patterns
- **Comparing exercises**: which exercise activates the target muscle more effectively

## Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)

### Gesture Recognition

Classifying hand/arm gestures from EMG patterns for:
- Prosthetic hand control (pattern recognition → grasp type)
- VR/AR input (hand gestures without cameras)
- Game controllers
- Silent speech recognition (subvocalization EMG from throat/face)

### Assistive Technology

- Wheelchair control via facial/neck EMG
- Communication devices for locked-in patients
- Switch access (binary on/off via any controllable muscle)

## Research

### Motor Control Studies

- Understanding how the nervous system coordinates movement
- Motor unit decomposition (HD-EMG) — identifying individual motor unit firing patterns
- Synergy analysis — finding coordinated muscle activation patterns

### Biomechanical Modeling

- EMG-driven musculoskeletal models (e.g., OpenSim)
- Estimating joint moments from muscle activations
- Forward dynamics simulation
